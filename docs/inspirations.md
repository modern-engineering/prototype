# Inspirations

Here we credit the people and projects whose work influenced the toolkit, from ideas we borrowed to ones we are still exploring.

## A field guide to similar birds (fictional example)

The imaginary *Pocket Guide to Shorebirds* puts two similar birds on facing pages and points out the easiest way to tell them apart.
If we borrowed that idea for command help, we might show two easily confused commands side by side and explain when to use each one.
